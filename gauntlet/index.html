How to Play Gauntlet

        

        
      

      

        

          Clear 5 bite-sized daily puzzles in a row using one shared health bar of 25 turns (⚡).
        


        

          

            ⚡
            

              One Shared 25-Turn Health Bar
              Every stage drains from the same 25⚡ pool. Finish all 5 stages before your bar hits 0!
            

          


          

            🎯
            

              Par Ratings & Turn Costs
              Each stage displays a target Par. Word, math, flip, pair & code games cost 1⚡ per action; sliding & swap puzzles (Slide, Waffle, Jam, Ice) grant 2 moves per 1⚡.
            

          


          

            ✨
            

              2 Rare +2⚡ Golden Boosts
              Only 2 stages each day hide a +2⚡ bonus objective. Grabbing them heals your turn bar!
            

          


          

            📅
            

              New Lineup Daily
              5 games are chosen from the 10-game roster each day. Progress auto-saves after every turn.
