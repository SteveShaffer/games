How to Play Gauntlet

        

        
      

      

        
Beat 5 short daily puzzles in a row using a single shared pool of 25 turns.

        

          

            
⚡

            

              One Shared 25-Turn Health Bar
              Every move consumes turns from your shared 25-turn pool across all 5 stages. Run out of turns and the gauntlet ends!
            

          

          

            
🎯

            

              Par Ratings & Turn Costs
              Single-action games cost 1⚡ per action. Deep grid games (Slide, Waffle, Jam, Ice, Sudoku, Mines, Knight, Bridges) grant 2 moves per 1⚡.
            

          

          

            
✨

            

              2 Rare +2⚡ Golden Boosts
              Exactly 2 hidden golden boosts appear across the 5 daily stages. Hunt them down to replenish your health bar.
            

          

          

            
📅

            

              New Lineup Daily
              A fresh 5-stage run drawn from our 15-game roster arrives every midnight. Your daily progress auto-saves as you play.
